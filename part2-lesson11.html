Grammar points on page 118

1

こ (これ・この・ここ・こう) — "this / here / like this"

  • For things near both the speaker and listener that both can see (①②), or things on the speaker's side (③④).
    1. これはだれのかばんですか。 Whose bag is this?
    2. A「この小ちいさいカメラは日本にほんのですね。」B「ええ。でも、これはちょっと高たかいですね。」 A: "This small camera is Japanese, isn't it?" B: "Yes. But this one is a bit expensive."
    3. ここがいたいんです。 It hurts here. (pointing to one's own body)
    4. すしはこうして作つくります。 You make sushi like this.
2

そ (それ・その・そこ・そう) — "that (near you) / there"

  • For things closer to the listener (①), things a little away from both (②), or things only one of the two people knows about (③④).
    1. サラさん、そのゆびわ、きれいですね。 Sarah, that ring (you're wearing) is pretty.
    2. トイレはそこです。 The toilet is right there.
    3. きのう高校こうこうの友ともだちと会あいました。その人ひとも来週らいしゅうのパーティーに来きます。 I met a high school friend yesterday. That person is coming to next week's party too.
    4. 「鳥とりよし」ですか。その店みせはどこにありますか。 "Toriyoshi"? Where is that shop? (the listener mentioned it; the speaker doesn't know it)
3

あ (あれ・あの・あそこ・ああ) — "that (over there)"

  • For things far from both people but visible to both (①②), or things both people know about (③④).
    1. あれが有名ゆうめいな東京とうきょうスカイツリーです。 That over there is the famous Tokyo Skytree.
    2. ほら、あの木きの上うえに鳥とりがいますよ。 Look, there's a bird on top of that tree.
    3. きのう行いった店みせはよかったですね。またあそこに行いきましょう。 The shop we went to yesterday was good. Let's go there again.
    4. A「林はやしさん、今日きょうからたばこをやめると言いっていましたよ。」B「あの人ひとはいつもああ言いいます。」 A: "Hayashi said they're quitting smoking from today." B: "That person always says that."

Quick tip: In conversation, if only one of you knows the thing, use そ. If you both know it, use あ.

Practice 15 questions
Instructions

「こ・そ・あ」の正ただしい形かたちを書かいてください。

Write the correct こ・そ・あ word.

Q1 Write

すみません。こ　でたばこをすってもいいですか。

Excuse me. May I smoke here?

Show answerHide answer
Answerここ

Grammarここ = here (where the speaker is)

The place the speaker is standing: ここで.

Sumimasen. Koko de tabako o sutte mo ii desu ka.

Q2 Write

①そ　本ほんは持もって帰かえらないで、②こ　で読よんでください。

Please don't take that book home; read it here.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① その　② ここ

Grammarその + noun; ここ = here

① The book is near the listener: その本ほん (その before a noun). ② Read it here, where the speaker is: ここで.

Sono hon wa motte kaeranaide, koko de yonde kudasai.

Q3 Write

客きゃく「あの、①こ　シャツのMサイズはありますか。」
店員てんいん「ああ、②そ　ならありますよ。ちょっと待まってください。」

Customer: "Um, do you have this shirt in a medium?"
Staff: "Ah, that one we have. Just a moment, please."

Show answerHide answer
Answer① この　② それ

Grammarこの (speaker's side) / それ (listener's side)

① The customer is holding the shirt: このシャツ. ② For the staff member, the shirt is near the customer: それ.

Kyaku: "Ano, kono shatsu no emu-saizu wa arimasu ka." Ten'in: "Aa, sore nara arimasu yo."

Q4 Write

あ　めがねをかけている人ひとはだれですか。

Who is that person over there wearing glasses?

Show answerHide answer
Answerあの

Grammarあの + noun (far from both)

A person over there: あの人ひと. あの comes before a noun.

Ano megane o kakete iru hito wa dare desu ka.

Q5 Write

バスの客きゃく「①あ　に高たかいビルがありますね。②こ　バスは③あ　へんまで行いきますか。」
運転手うんてんしゅ「ええ、行いきますよ。」

Bus passenger: "There's a tall building over there. Does this bus go as far as that area?"
Driver: "Yes, it does."

Show answerHide answer
Answer① あそこ　② この　③ あの

Grammarあそこ / この / あの

① A place far from both: あそこに. ② The bus they're on: このバス. ③ That area over there (before a noun): あのへん.

Basu no kyaku: "Asoko ni takai biru ga arimasu ne. Kono basu wa ano hen made ikimasu ka."

れんしゅう2

Practice 24 questions
Instructions

いちばんいいものをえらんでください。

Choose the best option.

Q1 Choose

先生せんせい「トムさん、（①　）時計とけい、新あたらしいですね。」
トム「ええ、（②　）は誕生日たんじょうびに兄あににもらったんです。」

Teacher: "Tom, that watch is new, isn't it?"
Tom: "Yes, I got this from my older brother for my birthday."

Show answerHide answer
Answer① c. その　② a. これ

Grammarその (listener's thing, before a noun) / これ (speaker's thing, alone)

① The watch is on Tom (the listener), and it's before a noun: その時計とけい. b それ can't come before a noun. ② For Tom, it's his own watch: これ (alone, no noun after).

Sensei: "Tomu-san, sono tokei, atarashii desu ne."
Tomu: "Ee, kore wa tanjoubi ni ani ni moratta n desu."

Q2 Choose

【レストランで】A「　のカレーはおいしいですね。」
B「そうですね。来週らいしゅうも来きましょう。」

[At a restaurant] A: "The curry at this restaurant is delicious."
B: "It is. Let's come again next week."

Show answerHide answer
Answera. この店みせ

Grammarこの = where both are right now

They're both in the restaurant: この店みせ.

A: "Kono mise no karee wa oishii desu ne."

Q3 Choose

A「先週せんしゅう、山田やまださんに会あいましたよ。」
B「山田やまださん？（①　）はだれですか。」
A「林はやしさんの友ともだちですよ。」
B「ああ、（②　）ですか。おもしろい人ひとですよね。」

A: "I met Yamada last week."
B: "Yamada? Who's that?"
A: "Hayashi's friend."
B: "Oh, that person! They're funny, aren't they?"

Show answerHide answer
Answer① b. その人ひと　② c. あの人ひと

Grammarそ (only one knows) → あ (both know)

① At first, only A knows Yamada: その人ひと. ② Once B remembers, both know them: あの人ひと.

B: "Yamada-san? Sono hito wa dare desu ka." … B: "Aa, ano hito desu ka. Omoshiroi hito desu yo ne."

Q4 Choose

うちのとなりに外国人がいこくじんが住すんでいます。　も日本にほん語ごを勉強べんきょうしています。

A foreigner lives next door to me. That person is also studying Japanese.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. その人ひと

Grammarそ for something just mentioned (only the speaker knows)

The speaker just introduced this person; the listener doesn't know them: その人ひと. a あの would mean both know them. c あそこの人ひと = "the person over there."

Uchi no tonari ni gaikokujin ga sunde imasu. Sono hito mo Nihongo o benkyou shite imasu.

Problem 16 questions
Instructions

　に何なんを入いれますか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in the ( )? Choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

A「ざっしを買かったんですか。」
B「ええ、電車でんしゃの中なかで読よむ　買かいました。」

A: "Did you buy a magazine?"
B: "Yes, I bought it intending to read it on the train."

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. つもりで

Grammarつもりで (with the intention of ~)

B bought it with a plan in mind: 読よむつもりで. 2 はず is a confident guess, not an intention. 3 ところで doesn't fit here. 4 The purpose form is ために, not ためで.

B: "Ee, densha no naka de yomu tsumori de kaimashita."

Q2 Choose

A「うーん。スケートはむずかしいね。うまくできない。」
B「だいじょうぶ。れんしゅうすれば、すぐに　なるよ。」

A: "Hmm. Skating is hard. I can't do it well."
B: "Don't worry. If you practise, you'll soon be able to skate."

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. すべれるように

GrammarPotential + ようになる (become able to)

A can't skate yet, so B says "you'll become able to skate": すべれるようになる. 1 すべるようになる = "start skating (as a habit)." 3 そうに = "look like." 4 のに = "even though."

B: "Daijoubu. Renshuu sureba, sugu ni subereru you ni naru yo." (すべる → potential すべれる)

Q3 Choose

A「あ、ここはボールペンで書かくんですか。」
B「ボールペンが　、えんぴつで書かいてもいいですよ。」

A: "Oh, do I write this part in ballpoint pen?"
B: "If you don't have a ballpoint pen, you can write in pencil."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. なかったら

Grammar～なかったら (if not ~) + permission

"If you don't have one, it's OK to use pencil." たら can be followed by permission (てもいい). 1 のに = "even though." 2 なくて is a reason. 4 なくては leads to "must."

B: "Boorupen ga nakattara, enpitsu de kaite mo ii desu yo."

Q4 Choose

つぎの電車でんしゃが何なん時じに　知しっていますか。

Do you know what time the next train comes?

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 来くるか

GrammarQuestion word + か (embedded question)

何なん時じ is a question word, so the embedded question ends with か. 1 たら = "if/when." 2 と = quote. 3 のは doesn't form a question.

Tsugi no densha ga nanji ni kuru ka shitte imasu ka.

Q5 Choose

もっと早はやくうちを　と思おもっていたけど、おそくなりました。

I'd been meaning to leave home earlier, but I ended up late.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 出でよう

GrammarVolitional + と思おもっていた (had intended)

The speaker's own intention: 出でようと思おもっていた. 1 出でると思おもっていた = "thought (someone) would leave." 3 and 4 are そう forms (look like / hearsay).

Motto hayaku uchi o deyou to omotte ita kedo, osoku narimashita.

Q6 Choose

この説明せつめいはよくわかりません。もっと　ほうがいいと思おもいます。

I don't really understand this explanation. I think it would be better to make it more detailed.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. くわしくした

Grammar～くする (make ~) + たほうがいい

Advice to deliberately change the explanation: くわしくしたほうがいい. 1 なった = it becomes detailed by itself, which doesn't fit advice. 3 and 4 don't connect to ほうがいい properly here.

Kono setsumei wa yoku wakarimasen. Motto kuwashiku shita hou ga ii to omoimasu.

Problem 23 questions
Instructions

★に入はいるものはどれですか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

Which piece goes in the ★ slot? Put the four pieces in the correct order and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

この近ちかくに 　 　 ★ 　 妹いもうとが言いっていました。

My younger sister said there seems to be a Spanish restaurant near here.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. らしい (order: 2 → 4 → 3 → 1)

Grammarらしい + と言いっていました

Full sentence: この近ちかくにスペイン料理りょうりの レストランがある らしい と妹いもうとが言いっていました。 ★ is the third slot: らしい.

Kono chikaku ni Supein ryouri no resutoran ga aru rashii to imouto ga itte imashita.

Q2 Choose

買かい物ものをするときは、ほんとうに 　 　 ★ 　 買かいなさい。

When you go shopping, think about whether you really need it before you buy.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 考かんがえて (order: 2 → 4 → 1 → 3)

Grammarかどうか / てから / なさい

Full sentence: ほんとうにいる かどうか 考かんがえて から買かいなさい。 いる here means "need" (要いる). ★ is the third slot: 考かんがえて.

Kaimono o suru toki wa, hontou ni iru ka dou ka kangaete kara kainasai.

Q3 Choose

A「少すこしやせましたか。どうしたんですか。」
B「そうですねえ。さいきん、前まえ 　 　 ★ 　 でしょう。」

A: "Have you lost a bit of weight? What happened?"
B: "Hmm. I guess it's because I don't eat as much as I used to recently."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. なった (order: 3 → 1 → 4 → 2)

Grammar前まえほど～なくなった (not as much as before)

Full sentence: さいきん、前まえほど 食たべなく なった からでしょう。 ★ is the third slot: なった.

B: "Sou desu nee. Saikin, mae hodo tabenaku natta kara deshou."

Problem 34 questions
Instructions

1 から 4 に何なんを入いれますか。文章ぶんしょうの意味いみを考かんがえて、1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in blanks 1 to 4? Think about the meaning of the passage and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

The passage ("Bargain Sales" by Sarah Smith):

バーゲンセール　サラ・スミス

日本にほんではすてきな物ものをたくさん売うっていますが、[1] 高たかくてなかなか買かえません。しかし、安やすく買かうためのチャンスがあります。バーゲンセールです。わたしも新あたらしいくつが [2]、今週末こんしゅうまつ、友ともだちといっしょにデパートのバーゲンに行いきます。わたしがほしかったくつは、バーゲンの前まえまで1万いちまん円えんでした。まだ [3] わかりませんが、安やすくなっていたら買かおうと思おもっています。

バーゲンセールは1月がつと7月がつにあることが多おおいですが、さいきんは前まえより少すこし早はやく始はじまるそうです。バーゲンの日ひには人ひとがおおぜいデパートへ行いくので、デパートはとてもこみます。高たかい物ものを買かいたくない人ひとが [4]。

Translation: Japan sells lots of lovely things, but [1] even if I want them, they're expensive and I can't easily buy them. However, there's a chance to buy things cheaply: bargain sales. I [2] want to buy new shoes, so this weekend I'm going to a department store sale with a friend. The shoes I wanted cost 10,000 yen before the sale. I don't know [3] whether they'll still be there, but if they're cheaper, I'm thinking of buying them. Bargain sales are often in January and July, but I hear that recently they start a little earlier than before. On sale days lots of people go to department stores, so they get very crowded. It seems that people who don't want to buy expensive things [4] have increased.

Q1 Choose

すてきな物ものをたくさん売うっていますが、[1] 高たかくてなかなか買かえません。

They sell lots of lovely things, but even if I want them, they're expensive and I can't easily buy them.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. ほしくても

Grammar～くても (even if ~)

"Even if I want them, I can't buy them" is a contrast: ほしくても. 1 and 2 そう ("look like wanting") is wrong for your own feelings. 4 ば ("if I want them") doesn't lead to "can't buy."

…hoshikute mo takakute nakanaka kaemasen.

Q2 Choose

わたしも新あたらしいくつが [2]、今週末こんしゅうまつ、友ともだちといっしょにデパートのバーゲンに行いきます。

I want to buy new shoes too, so I'm going to a department store sale with a friend this weekend.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 買かいたいので

Grammarので (reason)

Wanting shoes is the reason for going: 買かいたいので. 1 のに = "even though." 3 and 4 turn it into a noun phrase, which doesn't connect.

Watashi mo atarashii kutsu ga kaitai node, konshuumatsu, tomodachi to issho ni depaato no baagen ni ikimasu.

Q3 Choose

まだ [3] わかりませんが、安やすくなっていたら買かおうと思おもっています。

I don't know yet whether they'll still be there, but if they've become cheaper, I'm thinking of buying them.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. あるかどうか

Grammarかどうか (yes/no question, present)

"Whether they'll be there (at the sale)" is a yes/no question about the future: あるかどうか. 2 is past. 3 and 4 ask what is there, but she's asking about specific shoes.

Mada aru ka dou ka wakarimasen ga, yasuku natte itara kaou to omotte imasu.

Q4 Choose

高たかい物ものを買かいたくない人ひとが [4]。

It seems the number of people who don't want to buy expensive things has increased.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 多おおくなったようです

Grammar～くなる (change) + ようです (seems)

Crowded stores suggest a change: more such people now. 多おおくなったようです. 2 多おおくした = "someone made it more," which doesn't fit. 3 and 4 多おおかった ("there were many") don't show a change.

Takai mono o kaitakunai hito ga ooku natta you desu.

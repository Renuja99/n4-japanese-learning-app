Grammar points on page 64

1

～たら…

when ~ / after ~ happens

  • Form: verb た form + ら
  • "After ~ happens, do …." Here ~ is not a guess: it's something you know will happen (3 o'clock will come, you will graduate, the water will boil).
  • Examples from the book:
    1. 3時じになったら、休やすみましょう。 When it gets to 3 o'clock, let's take a break.
    2. この学校がっこうをそつぎょうしたら、何なんをしますか。 What will you do after you graduate from this school?
    3. おゆがわいたら、火ひを止とめてください。 When the water boils, please turn off the heat.
2

～なら…

if (it's the case that) ~ / if you're talking about ~

  • Form: plain form + なら (な-adj and nouns drop だ)
  • Picks up information from another person's words or situation, then gives the speaker's judgment, intention or suggestion about it.
  • Examples from the book:
    1. サラ「ケーキがおいしい店みせを知しりませんか。」山田やまだ「ケーキなら、駅前えきまえのセボンがおいしいですよ。」 Sarah: "Do you know a shop with good cake?" Yamada: "If it's cake you want, Seibon in front of the station is good."
    2. トム「春休はるやすみに京都きょうとに行いきたいです。」先生せんせい「京都きょうとに行いくなら、ガイドブックを貸かしましょうか。」 Tom: "I want to go to Kyoto in the spring break." Teacher: "If you're going to Kyoto, shall I lend you a guidebook?"
    3. 【テレビがついているが、子こどもは見みていない】母はは「見みていないなら、テレビはもう消けすよ。」 [The TV is on, but the child isn't watching] Mother: "If you're not watching it, I'm turning the TV off."

Quick tip: たら = after ~ happens. なら = "if that's the case" (based on what someone just said). With なら, the speaker's action can even happen before ~ (e.g. lend the guidebook before the trip).

Instructions

Change the word in the brackets ( ) into the correct form and write it in the blank.

Q1 Write

作文さくぶんをさいごまで　ら、見みせてください。（書かく）

When you've written your essay to the end, please show it to me.

Show answerHide answer
Answer書かいた（書かいたら）

Grammarた form + ら (after ~ happens)

書かく → 書かいた + ら.

Sakubun o saigo made kaitara, misete kudasai.

Q2 Write

うちへ　ら、少すこし休やすみます。（帰かえる）

Show answerHide answer
Answer帰かえった（帰かえったら）

Grammarた form + ら

帰かえる (Group I) → 帰かえった + ら.

Uchi e kaettara, sukoshi yasumimasu.

れんしゅう2

Practice 25 questions
Instructions

いちばんいいものをえらんでください。

Choose the best option.

Q1 Choose

この本ほん、おもしろいよ。読よんだら、　。

This book is interesting. When I've finished reading it, shall I lend it to you?

Show answerHide answer
Answerc. 貸かそうか

Grammarたら (after ~) + offer

"After I finish reading it, shall I lend it to you?" is a future offer. a 貸かしたよ is past ("I lent it"), which doesn't fit たら about the future. b 貸かしてね asks the other person to lend, but the speaker owns the book.

Kono hon, omoshiroi yo. Yondara, kasou ka.

Q2 Choose

ケーキを作つくるんですか。作つくったら、　。

You're making a cake? When you've made it, give me some too, OK?

Show answerHide answer
Answerc. わたしにもくださいね

Grammarたら (after ~ is done)

作つくったら = "after it's made." Asking for a piece fits after. a Offering to help belongs before/while making, not after. b Eggs and sugar are needed before making it.

Keeki o tsukuru n desu ka. Tsukuttara, watashi ni mo kudasai ne.

Q3 Choose

先生せんせい「サラさんを見みなかった？」
ジョー「　、さっきとなりのへやにいましたよ。」

Teacher: "Have you seen Sarah?"
Joe: "If it's Sarah you mean, she was in the next room a moment ago."

Show answerHide answer
Answera. サラさんなら

GrammarNoun + なら (picking up the topic)

Joe picks up the topic the teacher raised: "Sarah? (If it's Sarah,) she was next door." b and c don't make sense: Joe is the one who saw her.

Joo: "Sara-san nara, sakki tonari no heya ni imashita yo."

Q4 Choose

サラ「トム、あしたのパーティーに行いく？トムが　、わたしも行いく。」

Sarah: "Tom, are you going to tomorrow's party? If you're going, I'll go too."

Show answerHide answer
Answera. 行いくなら

Grammarなら (if that's the case) + speaker's intention

Sarah decides based on Tom's plan: "if you're going, then I'll go too." b 行いったら = "after you've gone," but they'd go together. c 行いったなら = "if you went (already)," the wrong timing.

Sara: "Tomu, ashita no paatii ni iku? Tomu ga iku nara, watashi mo iku."

Q5 Choose

先生せんせい「試験しけんが　、夏休なつやすみですから、それまでがんばってください。」

Teacher: "When the exams are over, it's summer vacation, so please work hard until then."

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 終おわったら

Grammarたら (after something that will surely happen)

The exams will definitely end, and then vacation starts: 終おわったら. a and c なら suggest "if it's the case that…," which doesn't fit a certain future event.

Sensei: "Shiken ga owattara, natsuyasumi desu kara, sore made ganbatte kudasai."

Problem 16 questions
Instructions

　に何なんを入いれますか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in the ( )? Choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

きのうは暑あつかったですが、今日きょうはきのう　少すこしすずしいですね。

It was hot yesterday, but today is a little cooler than yesterday, isn't it?

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. より

GrammarA は B より ~ (A is more ~ than B)

Comparing today with yesterday: 今日きょうはきのうより (more) すずしい. 1 から = "from" and 2 まで = "until" aren't for comparing. 3 ほど is used with a negative: きのうほど暑あつくない ("not as hot as yesterday"), but this sentence is positive.

Kinou wa atsukatta desu ga, kyou wa kinou yori sukoshi suzushii desu ne.

Q2 Choose

わたしは日本にほんに来くる　タイの会社かいしゃで働はたらいていました。

I worked at a Thai company until I came to Japan.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. まで

Grammarまで (until) with a continuing action

働はたらいていました is a continuing action that lasted up to coming to Japan, so まで. 2 までに is for a one-time action before a deadline. 3 までで and 4 までの (followed by a noun) don't fit before a verb phrase like this.

Watashi wa Nihon ni kuru made Tai no kaisha de hataraite imashita.

Q3 Choose

コーヒーを　ビデオを見みる時間じかんがとても好すきだ。

I really love the time when I watch videos while drinking coffee.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 飲のみながら

Grammarます-stem + ながら

Drinking coffee and watching videos happen at the same time: 飲のみながら見みる. 1 飲のむとき would need a different structure ("when I drink coffee"), and sounds odd directly before ビデオを見みる. 2 飲のんだり needs a matching ～たり (飲のんだり見みたりする). 4 ところで doesn't connect two simultaneous actions.

Koohii o nominagara bideo o miru jikan ga totemo suki da.

Q4 Choose

A「その仕事しごと、わたしがやりましょうか。」
B「あ、いいですか。じゃ、　。」

A: "Shall I do that job?"
B: "Oh, are you sure? Then please do it."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. やってください

GrammarAccepting an offer (ましょうか → てください)

A offers to do the work, and B accepts by asking A to do it: やってください. 1 やります = "I'll do it" (but A is doing it). 2 やれます = "can do." 4 やりましょう = "let's do it (together)."

A: "Sono shigoto, watashi ga yarimashou ka."
B: "A, ii desu ka. Ja, yatte kudasai."

Q5 Choose

A「ほら、　でしょう？鳥とりのなき声ごえが……。」
B「あ、ほんとうだ。ピピピ、ピピピとないていますね。」

A: "Listen, you can hear it, right? A bird singing…"
B: "Oh, you're right. It's chirping 'pi-pi-pi.'"

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 聞きこえる

Grammar聞きこえる (a sound naturally reaches your ears)

The bird's song is reaching their ears naturally: 聞きこえるでしょう？ ("You can hear it, right?"). 1 聞きく = to listen (on purpose). 3 聞きける = able to listen because of the situation. 4 聞きかれる = passive ("be asked/be heard by").

A: "Hora, kikoeru deshou? Tori no nakigoe ga…."
B: "A, hontou da. Pipipi, pipipi to naite imasu ne."

Q6 Choose

A「あ、この花はな、どこかで　。」
B「去年きょねんのぼったさくら山やまにたくさんさいていましたね。」

A: "Oh, I've seen this flower somewhere before."
B: "There were lots of them blooming on Mt. Sakura, which we climbed last year."

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 見みたことがあります

Grammarた form + ことがある (past experience)

A remembers seeing this flower before (last year): past experience = 見みたことがあります. 1 = "sometimes see." 3 = "about to see." 4 = "have just seen," but it was last year, not just now.

A: "A, kono hana, dokoka de mita koto ga arimasu."

Problem 23 questions
Instructions

★に入はいるものはどれですか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

Which piece goes in the ★ slot? Put the four pieces in the correct order and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

びじゅつかんへ 　 　 ★ 　 ところですよ。急いそいでください。

The 9 o'clock bus going to the art museum is just about to leave. Please hurry.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. バスが (order: 1 → 4 → 3 → 2)

GrammarDictionary form + ところです (just about to); verb describing a noun

Full sentence: びじゅつかんへ行いく 9時じの バスが 出でるところですよ。 "びじゅつかんへ行いく" and "9時じの" both describe バス. The bus is the subject (が), and 出でるところ = "about to leave." The ★ is the third slot, バスが.

Bijutsukan e iku kuji no basu ga deru tokoro desu yo. Isoide kudasai.

Q2 Choose

この子こはわたしが 　 　 ★ 　 でしょうか。

Will this child be able to stay at home all alone until I come back?

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 一人ひとりだけで (order: 2 → 4 → 3 → 1)

GrammarDictionary form + まで / potential form いられる

Full sentence: この子こはわたしが帰かえってくる まで 一人ひとりだけで 家いえにいられるでしょうか。 "Until I come back" (帰かえってくるまで), "by themselves" (一人ひとりだけで), "can stay at home" (家いえにいられる, potential of いる). ★ is the third slot: 一人ひとりだけで.

Kono ko wa watashi ga kaette kuru made hitori dake de ie ni irareru deshou ka.

Q3 Choose

A「もう昼ひるご飯はんを食たべましたか。駅前えきまえのラーメン屋やに行いきませんか。」
B「あの、今いま 　 　 ★ 　 ところなんです。」

A: "Have you had lunch yet? Would you like to go to the ramen shop in front of the station?"
B: "Um, I've just eaten a bento I bought at the convenience store."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. おべんとうを (order: 3 → 2 → 4 → 1)

Grammarた form + ところです (just finished)

Full sentence: 今いまコンビニで 買かった おべんとうを 食たべたところなんです。 "コンビニで買かった" describes おべんとう. 食たべたところ = "have just eaten," which is B's polite reason for saying no. ★ is the third slot: おべんとうを.

Ano, ima konbini de katta obentou o tabeta tokoro na n desu.

Problem 34 questions
Instructions

1 から 4 に何なんを入いれますか。文章ぶんしょうの意味いみを考かんがえて、1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in blanks 1 to 4? Think about the meaning of the passage and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

The passage (a letter from Sarah to her host mother):

お母かあさん、お元気げんきですか。ホームステイのときはありがとうございました。わたしは元気げんきですから、しんぱいしないでください。日本にほんの生活せいかつにももう [1]。毎日まいにちスーパーで買かい物ものをして、自分じぶんで料理りょうりを作つくっています。でも、まだ上手じょうずに [2]。料理りょうりの本ほんを見みながら作つくりますが、お母かあさんの料理りょうりほどおいしくないです。先週せんしゅうの日曜日にちようびに学校がっこうの友ともだちといっしょにてんぷらを作つくりました。写真しゃしんを見みてください。これがはじめて [3] わたしたちのてんぷらです。

夏休なつやすみは3週間しゅうかんだけですが、いろいろな計画けいかくを考かんがえています。その前まえに試験しけんがあります。試験しけんまで毎日まいにちいそがしいです。でも、その後あとは楽たのしい夏休なつやすみですから、[4]。試験しけんの後あとで、またお手紙てがみを書かきます。さようなら。　サラ

Translation: Dear Mom (host mother), how are you? Thank you for everything during my homestay. I'm doing well, so please don't worry. I've already [1] gotten used to life in Japan. Every day I shop at the supermarket and cook for myself. But I [2] can't cook well yet. I cook while looking at a cookbook, but it isn't as tasty as your cooking. Last Sunday I made tempura with friends from school. Please look at the photo. This is our tempura that we [3] made for the first time. Summer vacation is only three weeks, but I'm thinking about lots of plans. Before that, there's an exam. I'm busy every day until the exam. But after that it's a fun summer vacation, so [4] I'll do my best. I'll write you another letter after the exam. Goodbye. Sarah

Q1 Choose

日本にほんの生活せいかつにももう [1]。

I've already gotten used to life in Japan, too.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. なれました

Grammarもう + past tense (already done)

もう ("already") + a completed change: なれました ("have gotten used to"). 1 なれます = "will get used to." 3 = "about to get used to," which contradicts もう. 4 ている＋ところ is for an action in progress, which doesn't fit "getting used to" with もう.

Nihon no seikatsu ni mo mou naremashita.

Q2 Choose

でも、まだ上手じょうずに [2]。

But I can't make it well yet.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 作つくれません

GrammarPotential form (negative), present

まだ ("still / not yet") + present negative ability: "I can't make it well yet" = 作つくれません. 1 作つくりません = "I don't make it" (a choice). 2 and 4 are past tense, but she's talking about now (まだ).

Demo, mada jouzu ni tsukuremasen.

Q3 Choose

これがはじめて [3] わたしたちのてんぷらです。

This is our tempura that we made for the first time.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 作つくった

GrammarPlain past form describing a noun (はじめて作つくった〜)

はじめて作つくったてんぷら = "the tempura (we) made for the first time." 2 作つくっていた = "were making" (ongoing). 3 "have never made" contradicts the photo. 4 "sometimes make" doesn't fit はじめて.

Kore ga hajimete tsukutta watashitachi no tenpura desu.

Q4 Choose

でも、その後あとは楽たのしい夏休なつやすみですから、[4]。

But after that it's a fun summer vacation, so I'll do my best.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. がんばります

Grammarます form for your own intention ("I will ~")

Sarah is talking about her own effort for the exam: がんばります ("I'll work hard"). 2 がんばりましょう = "let's both work hard," but the mother isn't taking the exam. 3 がんばってください would tell the mother to work hard. 4 is past tense, but the exam is in the future.

Demo, sono ato wa tanoshii natsuyasumi desu kara, ganbarimasu.

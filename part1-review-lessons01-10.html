Problem 16 questions
Instructions

　に何なんを入いれますか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in the ( )? Choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

A「今いまはよやくだけしてください。お金かねは後あとでもいいですよ。」
B「あ、今いま　。よかった。」

A: "For now, please just make the reservation. You can pay later."
B: "Oh, so I don't have to pay now? Great."

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 払はらわなくてもいいですか

Grammar～なくてもいい (no need)

A said payment can wait, so B checks: "I don't have to pay now?" and is relieved (よかった). 1 "May I pay?" and 3 "Mustn't I pay?" don't match the relief. 4 "Do I have to pay?" contradicts what A just said.

B: "A, ima harawanakute mo ii desu ka. Yokatta."

Q2 Choose

ゆうびんきょくの人ひと「スミスさん、にもつですよ。はんこをおねがいします。」
スミス「はんこがありません。サイン　いいですか。」

Post office worker: "Mr./Ms. Smith, a parcel for you. Your seal (stamp), please."
Smith: "I don't have a seal. Is a signature OK?"

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. でも

GrammarNoun + でもいいですか (Is ~ OK instead?)

Noun + でもいい = "is ~ acceptable (even though it's not what you asked for)?" 2 ではいけません is the prohibition pattern, not used with いい. 1 and 4 don't make the permission pattern.

Sumisu: "Hanko ga arimasen. Sain demo ii desu ka."

Q3 Choose

あれ？あの子こはこんな所ところにかばんを　、どこへ行いったんだろう。

Huh? That kid left their bag here and went off somewhere. Where did they go?

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. おいたまま

Grammarた form + まま (left as is)

The bag was left in place (an unchanged state), and the child disappeared: おいたまま. 2 "because they put it" doesn't make sense. 3 ながら needs two simultaneous actions. 4 "in order to put it" doesn't fit.

Are? Ano ko wa konna tokoro ni kaban o oita mama, doko e itta n darou.

Q4 Choose

A「このりんご、まだ少すこし青あおいですよね。だいじょうぶでしょうか。」
B「だいじょうぶ。もう　そうですよ。」

A: "This apple is still a bit green, isn't it? Is it OK?"
B: "It's fine. It looks like it can be eaten already."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 食たべられ

GrammarPotential stem + そうです

"It looks edible" = 食たべられそう. 1 食たべそう = "looks like (someone) is about to eat." 2 食たべたそう is hearsay ("I heard someone ate"). 4 食たべたがりそう doesn't make sense for an apple.

B: "Daijoubu. Mou taberaresou desu yo."

Q5 Choose

火ひを　いい料理りょうりはかんたんでいいですよね。

Dishes you can make without using the stove (fire) are easy, which is nice.

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 使つかわなくても

Grammar～なくてもいい (no need) describing a noun

火ひを使つかわなくてもいい料理りょうり = "dishes that don't need fire." That's why they're easy. 3 使つかってもいい = "dishes you're allowed to use fire for," which doesn't explain why they're easy. 1 and 2 don't fit before いい here.

Hi o tsukawanakute mo ii ryouri wa kantan de ii desu yo ne.

Q6 Choose

来週らいしゅう、ちょっとわたしの仕事しごとを　来きてくれませんか。

Could you come and help me with my work a bit next week?

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 手伝てつだいに

Grammarます-stem + に来くる (purpose of movement)

"Come in order to help" = 手伝てつだいに来くる. 1 手伝てつだって来きて = "help and then come." 3 ので is a reason. 4 ように is for non-volitional verbs.

Raishuu, chotto watashi no shigoto o tetsudai ni kite kuremasen ka.

Problem 23 questions
Instructions

★に入はいるものはどれですか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

Which piece goes in the ★ slot? Put the four pieces in the correct order and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

ジョンさんに会あえて 　 　 ★ 　 2時間じかんも話はなしました。

I was so happy to see John that we talked for two whole hours, still standing.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 立たった (order: 2 → 3 → 1 → 4)

Grammar～ので (reason) + た form + まま

Full sentence: ジョンさんに会あえてうれしかった ので 立たった まま 2時間じかんも話はなしました。 "I was happy, so (without sitting down) we talked standing for two hours." ★ is the third slot: 立たった.

Jon-san ni aete ureshikatta node tatta mama nijikan mo hanashimashita.

Q2 Choose

A「ぼく、一人ひとりで住すみたいと思おもって、今いまへやをさがしているんだ。」
B「そう、　 　 ★ 　 ね。」

A: "I want to live by myself, so I'm looking for a room now."
B: "I see. I hope you find a good one."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 見みつかると (order: 2 → 4 → 3 → 1)

Grammar～といい (I hope ~)

Full sentence: そう、いいの が 見みつかると いいね。 いいの = "a good one." 見みつかるといいね = "I hope you find it." ★ is the third slot: 見みつかると.

B: "Sou, ii no ga mitsukaru to ii ne."

Q3 Choose

A「このへや、午前ごぜんの会議かいぎのままですね。かたづけましょうか。」
B「後あとで旅行りょこうの 　 　 ★ 　 かたづけなくてもいいよ。」

A: "This room is still as it was after the morning meeting. Shall I tidy it?"
B: "We'll use it later to discuss the trip, so you don't need to tidy it."

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 使つかう (order: 1 → 3 → 2 → 4)

GrammarDictionary form + ために (purpose) / ～から (reason) / ～なくてもいい

Full sentence: 後あとで旅行りょこうのそうだんをする ために 使つかう から かたづけなくてもいいよ。 "We'll use (the room) to discuss the trip, so no need to tidy." ★ is the third slot: 使つかう.

B: "Ato de ryokou no soudan o suru tame ni tsukau kara katazukenakute mo ii yo."

Problem 34 questions
Instructions

1 から 4 に何なんを入いれますか。文章ぶんしょうの意味いみを考かんがえて、1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in blanks 1 to 4? Think about the meaning of the passage and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

The passage (a notice to the neighbourhood):

ご近所きんじょのみなさんへ

みなさんはもう着きない服ふく、[1] おさらやなべ、かびんなどをどうしますか。おく場所ばしょがなくて、すてたいと思おもったことはありませんか。[2]、ちょっと待まってください。すててはいけません。あなたが使つかわない物ものを [3] 売うることができます。それがフリーマーケットです。フリーマーケットはいらない物ものを売うる所ところです。そして、ほしい物ものが安やすいねだんで買かえる所ところです。フリーマーケットは月つきに一度いちど、こうえんの中なかで開ひらきます。売うる物ものは [4]。新あたらしくなくてもいいです。

みなさん、フリーマーケットに店みせを出だしてみませんか。

Translation: To everyone in the neighbourhood: What do you do with clothes you no longer wear, and plates, pots, vases and so on that [1] you don't look likely to use? Have you ever wanted to throw them away because you had no space to keep them? [2] But wait a moment. Don't throw them away. You can sell things you don't use [3] to people who want to use them. That's a flea market. A flea market is a place to sell things you don't need, and a place where you can buy things you want at low prices. The flea market is held once a month in the park. [4] Anything is fine to sell. It doesn't have to be new. Everyone, why not try setting up a stall at the flea market?

Q1 Choose

みなさんはもう着きない服ふく、[1] おさらやなべ、かびんなどをどうしますか。

What do you do with clothes you no longer wear, and plates, pots and vases that it seems you'll never use?

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 使つかいそうもない

Grammar～そうもない (doesn't look likely to ~)

It matches "clothes you no longer wear": things you don't seem likely to use. 1 "look usable" and 2 "want to use" are the opposite. 4 "have used before" doesn't explain wanting to get rid of them.

…tsukaisou mo nai osara ya nabe, kabin nado o dou shimasu ka.

Q2 Choose

[2]、ちょっと待まってください。すててはいけません。

But wait a moment. Don't throw them away.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. でも

GrammarConjunctions: でも (but)

The previous sentence says you might want to throw things away; this one says "don't." That's a contrast, so でも. 1 だから = "so," 3 それから = "and then," 4 そして = "and" don't show contrast.

Demo, chotto matte kudasai. Sutete wa ikemasen.

Q3 Choose

あなたが使つかわない物ものを [3] 売うることができます。

You can sell the things you don't use to people who want to use them.

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 使つかいたい人ひとに

GrammarPerson + に売うる (sell to someone)

You sell to someone: 人ひとに売うる. The buyers are people who want to use the items. 1 "to people who used them" doesn't make sense. 2 でも changes the meaning. 3 が would make them the sellers.

Anata ga tsukawanai mono o tsukaitai hito ni uru koto ga dekimasu.

Q4 Choose

売うる物ものは [4]。新あたらしくなくてもいいです。

Anything is fine to sell. It doesn't have to be new.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 何なんでもいいです

Grammar何なんでも (anything)

The next sentence "it doesn't have to be new" fits "anything is OK." 2 and 3 are questions, which don't fit a notice. 4 "there's nothing" contradicts the idea.

Uru mono wa nan demo ii desu. Atarashikunakute mo ii desu.

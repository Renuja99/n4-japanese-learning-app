Grammar points on page 94

1

Uses of で

  • A. Place where an action or event happens.
    1. ろうかでえをかきました。 I drew a picture in the hallway.
    2. 近所きんじょで火事かじがありました。 There was a fire in the neighbourhood.
  • B. Method / means (①) or material (②).
    1. 毎日まいにち自転車じてんしゃで会社かいしゃに行いきます。 I go to work by bicycle every day.
    2. 紙かみでにんぎょうを作つくりました。 I made a doll out of paper.
  • C. Scope / range for a comparison.
    1. 日本にほんでいちばん高たかい山やまは富士山ふじさんです。 The highest mountain in Japan is Mt. Fuji.
  • D. Cause or reason.
    1. 父ちちは今いま仕事しごとでいそがしいです。 My father is busy with work right now.
    2. かぜで学校がっこうへ行いけませんでした。 I couldn't go to school because of a cold.
2

Uses of に

  • A. Where things or living beings exist (①), or where a state can be seen (②).
    1. あそこに池いけがあるでしょう？あの池いけに魚さかながたくさんいますよ。 There's a pond over there, right? There are lots of fish in that pond.
    2. にわにいろいろな花はながさいていますね。 All kinds of flowers are blooming in the garden.
  • B. Destination: where an object ends up (①) or where the subject moves to (②).
    1. ろうかにえをかきました。 I drew a picture on the hallway (wall/floor).
    2. 毎朝まいあさバスに乗のります。 I get on the bus every morning.
  • C. The person or thing receiving an action.
    1. あしたこの本ほんを山中やまなかさんに返かえします。 I'll return this book to Yamanaka tomorrow.
    2. その写真しゃしん、ちょっとわたしに見みせてよ。 Show me that photo for a second.
  • D. A point in time.
    1. じゅぎょうは9時じに始はじまります。 Class starts at 9.

Quick tip: Compare ろうかでえをかいた (I drew a picture while in the hallway, e.g. on paper) and ろうかにえをかいた (I drew a picture onto the hallway itself).

れんしゅう1

Practice 110 questions
Instructions

　の中なかに「で」か「に」を書かいてください。

Write で or に in the brackets.

Q1 Write

子こどものへや　大おおきいまどを作つくりました。へやが明あかるくなりました。

We put a big window in the child's room. The room became bright.

Show answerHide answer
Answerに

Grammarに = where the result ends up

The window now exists in the room, so に (destination/result).

Kodomo no heya ni ookii mado o tsukurimashita.

Q2 Write

子こどものへや　いっしょに紙かみのひこうきを作つくりました。

We made paper airplanes together in the child's room.

Show answerHide answer
Answerで

Grammarで = place of action

The room is just where the activity happened, so で.

Kodomo no heya de issho ni kami no hikouki o tsukurimashita.

Q3 Write

じこ　電車でんしゃが止とまっています。

The trains have stopped because of an accident.

Show answerHide answer
Answerで

Grammarで = cause

The accident is the reason: じこで.

Jiko de densha ga tomatte imasu.

Q4 Write

東京駅とうきょうえき（①　）地下鉄ちかてつ（②　）乗のりかえます。

I change to the subway at Tokyo Station.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① で　② に

Grammarで = place of action; に = what you get on

You transfer at the station (で) onto the subway (に).

Toukyou-eki de chikatetsu ni norikaemasu.

Q5 Write

風かぜ（①　）外そとのせんたく物ものがとびそうです。中なか（②　）入いれてください。

The laundry outside looks like it's going to blow away in the wind. Please bring it inside.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① で　② に

Grammarで = cause; に = destination

Because of the wind (で); bring it into the inside (に).

Kaze de soto no sentakumono ga tobisou desu. Naka ni irete kudasai.

Q6 Write

あしたホール（①　）お茶ちゃの会かいがあります。3時じ（②　）ホール（③　）集あつまってください。

There's a tea gathering in the hall tomorrow. Please gather at the hall at 3 o'clock.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① で　② に　③ に

Grammarで = place of an event; に = time point; に = destination

An event happens in the hall (で). 3時じに is a time point. Gather to the hall (に).

Ashita hooru de ocha no kai ga arimasu. Sanji ni hooru ni atsumatte kudasai.

Q7 Write

むこう（①　）高たかい山やまが見みえるでしょう？今日きょうはあの山やま（②　）のぼります。

You can see a tall mountain over there, right? Today we're going to climb that mountain.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① に　② に

Grammarに = location of existence; に = destination

The mountain exists over there (に). Climb up onto the mountain (山やまにのぼる).

Mukou ni takai yama ga mieru deshou? Kyou wa ano yama ni noborimasu.

Q8 Write

こちらのへや（①　）、このいす（②　）すわって少すこし休やすんでください。

Please sit on this chair in this room and rest a little.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① で　② に

Grammarで = place of action; に = where you sit

Rest in the room (で). Sit on the chair (いすにすわる).

Kochira no heya de, kono isu ni suwatte sukoshi yasunde kudasai.

Q9 Write

友ともだちの家いえ（①　）にもつを送おくったけど、そこ（②　）はもう友ともだちは住すんでいないようだった。

I sent a parcel to my friend's house, but it seems my friend doesn't live there any more.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① に　② に

Grammarに = destination; に with 住すむ

Send to the house (に). 住すむ always takes に for the place: そこには住すんでいない.

Tomodachi no ie ni nimotsu o okutta kedo, soko ni wa mou tomodachi wa sunde inai you datta.

Q10 Write

この紙かみ（①　）黒くろいペン（②　）名前なまえを書かいてください。

Please write your name on this paper with a black pen.

Show answerHide answer
Answer① に　② で

Grammarに = where something is written; で = tool

Write onto the paper (に) using a pen (で).

Kono kami ni kuroi pen de namae o kaite kudasai.

Practice 210 questions
Instructions

aかbか いいほうをえらんでください。

Choose the better option, a or b.

Q1 Choose

こうえんで（a 池いけが　b コンサートが）あります。

There's a concert in the park.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. コンサートが

Grammarで + ある (event happens)

で + ある is for events taking place. A concert is an event. a A pond just exists, which needs に (こうえんに池いけがある).

Kouen de konsaato ga arimasu.

Q2 Choose

ここに（a にもつをおいて　b 料理りょうりを作つくって）ください。

Please put your luggage here.

Show answerHide answer
Answera. にもつをおいて

Grammarに = where something is placed

に marks where the luggage ends up. b Cooking happens at a place, which needs で (ここで料理りょうりを作つくる).

Koko ni nimotsu o oite kudasai.

Q3 Choose

台風たいふうで（a はしがこわれました　b はしをわたりました）。

The bridge was broken by the typhoon.

Show answerHide answer
Answera. はしがこわれました

Grammarで = cause

The typhoon caused the damage. b "I crossed the bridge because of the typhoon" doesn't make sense.

Taifuu de hashi ga kowaremashita.

Q4 Choose

図書館としょかんに本ほんを（a かえします　b さがします）。

I'll return the book to the library.

Show answerHide answer
Answera. かえします

Grammarに = receiver / destination

The library receives the book (に). b Looking for a book happens in the library, which needs で.

Toshokan ni hon o kaeshimasu.

Q5 Choose

このホテルに（a いちばんいいへやはどこですか　b とてもいいへやがあります）。

There's a very nice room in this hotel.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. とてもいいへやがあります

Grammarに + ある (existence) vs. で (scope)

に + ある = something exists there. a "Which is the best room in this hotel?" is a comparison within a range, which needs で (このホテルで).

Kono hoteru ni totemo ii heya ga arimasu.

Q6 Choose

駅えきの前まえに（a 花屋はなやができました　b 花はなを買かいました）。

A flower shop has opened in front of the station.

Show answerHide answer
Answera. 花屋はなやができました

Grammarに = where something comes to exist

The flower shop now exists there (に). b Buying is an action at a place, which needs で.

Eki no mae ni hanaya ga dekimashita.

Q7 Choose

いつも（a 8時間じかんに　b 8時じに）ねます。

I always go to bed at 8 o'clock.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 8時じに

Grammarに = time point

に marks a point in time: 8時じに. a 8時間じかん is a length of time ("8 hours"), which doesn't take に here.

Itsumo hachiji ni nemasu.

Q8 Choose

動物園どうぶつえんにいろいろな（a 動物どうぶつがいます　b 動物どうぶつを見みましょう）。

There are all kinds of animals at the zoo.

Show answerHide answer
Answera. 動物どうぶつがいます

Grammarに + いる (existence)

に + いる = animals exist there. b Seeing animals is an action at a place, which needs で.

Doubutsuen ni iroiro na doubutsu ga imasu.

Q9 Choose

このみかんで（a むしが食たべました　b ジュースを作つくりましょう）。

Let's make juice with these mandarins.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. ジュースを作つくりましょう

Grammarで = material

The mandarins are the material for the juice. a "Insects ate (it) with these mandarins" doesn't make sense.

Kono mikan de juusu o tsukurimashou.

Q10 Choose

世界せかいで（a いろいろな国くにがあります　b いちばん広ひろい国くにはどこですか）。

Which is the largest country in the world?

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. いちばん広ひろい国くにはどこですか

Grammarで = scope for comparison

"The biggest in the world" is a comparison within a range: 世界せかいで. a Existence needs に (世界せかいにいろいろな国くにがある).

Sekai de ichiban hiroi kuni wa doko desu ka.

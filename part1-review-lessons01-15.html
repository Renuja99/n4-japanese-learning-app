Problem 16 questions
Instructions

　に何なんを入いれますか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in the ( )? Choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

その魚さかなは　かもしれませんよ。ちょっと悪わるくなっているようですから。

It might be better not to eat that fish. It seems to have gone a little bad.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 食たべないほうがいい

Grammarないほうがいい + かもしれない (softened advice)

The fish seems bad, so the advice is "better not eat it." かもしれません softens it. 1 "you may eat it" and 3 "you must eat it" contradict the warning. 4 "have never eaten" doesn't connect to the reason.

Sono sakana wa tabenai hou ga ii kamo shiremasen yo. Chotto waruku natte iru you desu kara.

Q2 Choose

今日きょうは仕事しごとが多おおくて　、もうねよう。

I had a lot of work today and I'm tired, so let's go to bed now.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. つかれたし

Grammar～し (listing reasons) + intention

ねよう ("let's sleep") is an intention, and て-reasons can't be followed by intentions. し can list reasons before an intention: "lots of work, (and) I'm tired, so let's sleep." 1 て can't come before ねよう. 3 "if I get tired" doesn't fit. 4 たり needs a pair and する.

Kyou wa shigoto ga ookute tsukareta shi, mou neyou.

Q3 Choose

母はは「このまんが、もうすてるよ。」
子こ「あ、　。ぼく、まだ読よむから。」

Mother: "I'm throwing this manga away now."
Child: "Oh, don't throw it away! I'm still reading it."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. すてないで

Grammar～ないで (please don't ~)

The child asks the mother not to throw it out: すてないで. 1 "throw it away!" is the opposite. 2 すてると is an unfinished "if/when you throw it." 4 すてないと = "you've got to throw it away."

Ko: "A, sutenaide. Boku, mada yomu kara."

Q4 Choose

A「京都駅きょうとえきはつぎだよ。」
B「京都きょうとに　、すぐ食事しょくじをしようね。」

A: "Kyoto Station is next."
B: "When we arrive in Kyoto, let's eat right away."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 着ついたら

Grammarたら (after something that will surely happen)

They will definitely arrive (it's the next stop), and then eat: 着ついたら. 1 "because we arrived" is past. 2 し lists reasons. 3 着ついたなら = "if it's true we've arrived," which doesn't fit.

B: "Kyouto ni tsuitara, sugu shokuji o shiyou ne."

Q5 Choose

あ、だめですよ。プールに入はいるときは、イヤリングを　。

Oh, you can't do that. When you go in the pool, you have to take your earrings off.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. とらないと

Grammar～ないと (you need to ~)

だめですよ ("that's not allowed") shows a rule: "you've got to take them off." 3 とらなくても(いい) = "you don't have to," the opposite. 2 and 4 are reasons, which leave the sentence unfinished.

A, dame desu yo. Puuru ni hairu toki wa, iyaringu o toranai to.

Q6 Choose

サラ「自転車じてんしゃを買かいたいんだけど、いい店みせを知しらない？」
トム「あ、自転車じてんしゃがほしい　、ぼくのをあげる。」

Sarah: "I want to buy a bicycle. Do you know a good shop?"
Tom: "Oh, if you want a bike, I'll give you mine."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. なら

Grammarなら (responding to what someone said)

Tom picks up Sarah's info ("you want a bike") and makes an offer: ほしいなら. 2 から ("because you want one") sounds odd here. 3 と can't be followed by the speaker's intention. 1 まま doesn't make sense.

Tomu: "A, jitensha ga hoshii nara, boku no o ageru."

Problem 23 questions
Instructions

★に入はいるものはどれですか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

Which piece goes in the ★ slot? Put the four pieces in the correct order and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

A「ぼくは毎日まいにちビールを3本ぽん飲のんで、ご飯はんを3ばい食たべるんだよ。」
B「え、そんなに 　 　 ★ 　 したほうがいいよ。」

A: "I drink three beers and eat three bowls of rice every day."
B: "What? You'd better not eat and drink so much, and do a bit of exercise instead."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. しないで (order: 2 → 4 → 3 → 1)

Grammar～たり～たりしないで (instead of doing ~) + ほうがいい

Full sentence: そんなにたくさん 飲のんだり食たべたり しないで 少すこし運動うんどうしたほうがいいよ。 ～ないで here means "instead of / without doing." ★ is the third slot: しないで.

B: "E, sonna ni takusan nondari tabetari shinaide sukoshi undou shita hou ga ii yo."

Q2 Choose

店長てんちょうは 　 　 ★ 　 ぼくたちも早はやく帰かえろう。

It seems the manager has already gone home, so when our work is finished, let's go home early too.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 仕事しごとが (order: 2 → 4 → 1 → 3)

Grammarようだ (seems) + から / たら

Full sentence: 店長てんちょうはもう帰かえった ようだから 仕事しごとが 終おわったらぼくたちも早はやく帰かえろう。 ★ is the third slot: 仕事しごとが.

Tenchou wa mou kaetta you da kara shigoto ga owattara bokutachi mo hayaku kaerou.

Q3 Choose

A「ジョンさんは今日きょう来きませんでしたね。」
B「ええ、けっこんしきに出でるために 　 　 ★ 　 です。」

A: "John didn't come today, did he?"
B: "No. He went back to his country to attend a wedding, so he shouldn't be in Japan."

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 日本にほんにいない (order: 1 → 4 → 2 → 3)

Grammar～から (reason) + はずです (logical conclusion)

Full sentence: けっこんしきに出でるために国くにへ帰かえった から 日本にほんにいない はずです。 The reason (went home) leads to a confident conclusion (はず). ★ is the third slot: 日本にほんにいない.

B: "Ee, kekkonshiki ni deru tame ni kuni e kaetta kara Nihon ni inai hazu desu."

Problem 34 questions
Instructions

1 から 4 に何なんを入いれますか。文章ぶんしょうの意味いみを考かんがえて、1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in blanks 1 to 4? Think about the meaning of the passage and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

The passage (a note from Lisa to Sarah):

サラさんへ

今日きょう、サラさんは来こられなかったけれど、みんなで来月らいげつの旅行りょこうのそうだんをしました。きまったことを [1]。

1. 朝あさ、6時じ40分ふんに学校がっこうの前まえに集あつまります。バスは7時じに出発しゅっぱつします。出発しゅっぱつが [2]、道みちがこむから、かならず6時じ40分ふんまでに来きてください。

2. 朝あさ、急きゅうに体からだのぐあいが悪わるくなったら、すぐ学校がっこうに電話でんわをしてください。

3. レストランで昼ひるご飯はんを食たべます。ですから、おべんとうは持もって [3]。

4. 山やまの上うえは風かぜも [4]、雨あめもふるかもしれません。かさとあたたかい上着うわぎがひつようです。

リサ

Translation: To Sarah: You couldn't come today, but we all discussed next month's trip. I'll [1] write down what we decided. 1. We'll meet in front of the school at 6:40 a.m. The bus leaves at 7. [2] If departure is late, the roads get crowded, so be sure to come by 6:40. 2. If you suddenly feel unwell in the morning, call the school right away. 3. We'll eat lunch at a restaurant. So you [3] don't need to bring a lunch box. 4. On top of the mountain it's [4] windy, and it might rain too. You'll need an umbrella and a warm jacket. — Lisa

Q1 Choose

きまったことを [1]。

I'll write down what was decided.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 書かきます

Grammarます form for your own action ("I will")

Lisa says what she's about to do: 書かきます. 2 "looks like I'll write" is a guess. 3 "won't you write?" is an invitation. 4 なさい is an order.

Kimatta koto o kakimasu.

Q2 Choose

出発しゅっぱつが [2]、道みちがこむから、かならず6時じ40分ふんまでに来きてください。

If the departure is late, the roads will get crowded, so be sure to come by 6:40.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. おくれると

Grammar～と (when ~, … naturally happens)

"When departure is late, the roads (naturally) get crowded": a natural result, so と. 1 おくれて is a reason/sequence, which reads oddly here. 3 "if it isn't late" is the opposite. 4 "without being late" doesn't fit.

Shuppatsu ga okureru to, michi ga komu kara, kanarazu rokuji yonjuppun made ni kite kudasai.

Q3 Choose

レストランで昼ひるご飯はんを食たべます。ですから、おべんとうは持もって [3]。

We'll eat lunch at a restaurant. So you don't need to bring a lunch box.

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. いかなくてもいいです

Grammar～なくてもいい (no need)

Lunch is at a restaurant, so there's no need to bring food: 持もっていかなくてもいいです. 1 "might not bring" and 3 "shouldn't be bringing" are guesses, not instructions. 2 "must bring" contradicts the restaurant plan.

Desu kara, obentou wa motte ikanakute mo ii desu.

Q4 Choose

山やまの上うえは風かぜも [4]、雨あめもふるかもしれません。

On top of the mountain it's windy, and it might rain too.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 強つよいし

Grammar～も～し、～も (listing reasons)

風かぜも…雨あめも lists two reasons for needing an umbrella and jacket, so し. 2 ので would make wind the cause of rain. 3 と and 4 たら make conditions, which don't fit the も…も pattern.

Yama no ue wa kaze mo tsuyoi shi, ame mo furu kamo shiremasen.

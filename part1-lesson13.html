Grammar points on page 60

1

～なさい

Do ~! — instruction

  • Form: verb ます-stem + なさい
  • Used by teachers to students and parents to children when giving instructions. Also used in exam instructions (③).
  • Examples from the book:
    1. 子こどもは早はやくねなさい。 Children, go to bed early.
    2. しゅくだいを出だしなさい。 Hand in your homework.
    3. つぎの言葉ことばを漢字かんじで書かきなさい。 Write the following words in kanji.
2

～ほうがいいです

you'd better ~ / it's better to ~

  • Form: verb た form / ない form + ほうがいいです
  • Gives advice, often when something bad might happen if you don't follow it. Often ends with よ or ね.
  • Examples from the book:
    1. 会場かいじょうに行いく前まえに地図ちずで場所ばしょをしらべたほうがいいですね。 You'd better check the location on a map before going to the venue.
    2. 寒さむいから、コートを着きたほうがいいよ。 It's cold, so you'd better wear a coat.
    3. ねつがあるの？じゃ、出でかけないほうがいいよ。 You have a fever? Then you'd better not go out.
3

～ないと

I've got to ~ / You need to ~

  • Form: verb ない form + と
  • Used to push yourself or someone else to act, because things will go badly if nothing is done. Casual spoken Japanese (short for ～ないといけない).
  • Examples from the book:
    1. けん、ご飯はんの前まえには手てをあらわないと。 Ken, you need to wash your hands before eating.
    2. あ、わすれていた。電話でんわしないと。 Oh, I forgot. I've got to make a phone call.
    3. 早はやく起おきて。ほら、急いそがないと。間まに合あわないよ。 Get up! Come on, you've got to hurry. You won't make it in time.

Instructions

　の中なかの言葉ことばを正ただしい形かたちにして、書かいてください。

Change the word in the brackets ( ) into the correct form and write it in the blank.

Q1 Write

けん、ほんとうのことを　なさい。（言いう）

Ken, tell the truth.

Show answerHide answer
Answer言いい（言いいなさい）

Grammarます-stem + なさい

言いいます → 言いい + なさい. A parent speaking to a child.

Ken, hontou no koto o iinasai.

Q2 Write

空そらが暗くらいので、かさを　ほうがいいですね。（持もっていく）

The sky is dark, so you'd better take an umbrella.

Show answerHide answer
Answer持もっていった（持もっていったほうがいい）

Grammarた form + ほうがいい

Positive advice uses the た form: 持もっていく → 持もっていった.

Sora ga kurai node, kasa o motte itta hou ga ii desu ne.

Q3 Write

あまりたばこを　ほうがいいですよ。（すう）

You'd better not smoke too much.

Show answerHide answer
Answerすわない（すわないほうがいい）

Grammarない form + ほうがいい

Negative advice uses the ない form: すう → すわない (う → わない).

Amari tabako o suwanai hou ga ii desu yo.

Q4 Write

けん、10時じよ。もうゲームを　と。（やめる）

Ken, it's 10 o'clock. You need to stop playing games now.

Show answerHide answer
Answerやめない（やめないと）

Grammarない form + と

やめる → やめない + と = "you need to stop."

Ken, juuji yo. Mou geemu o yamenai to.

Practice 28 questions
Instructions

いちばんいいものをえらんでください。

Choose the best option.

Q1 Choose

トム「サラ、しょうらいの仕事しごとのことはもっとよく　。」
サラ「そうだね。」

Tom: "Sarah, you should think more carefully about your future job."
Sarah: "You're right."

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 考かんがえたほうがいいよ

Grammarほうがいい (advice between friends)

Tom is giving friendly advice: ほうがいいよ. a なさい is for parents/teachers to children/students, not between friends. c ましょうか is an offer or suggestion to do it together.

Tomu: "Sara, shourai no shigoto no koto wa motto yoku kangaeta hou ga ii yo."

Q2 Choose

あ、山田やまださん、わたしもいっしょに帰かえります。ちょっと　。

Oh, Mr./Ms. Yamada, I'll go home with you. Please wait a moment.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 待まってください

Grammarてください (polite request)

It's a polite request to an adult: 待まってください. a なさい is an order from a parent/teacher, which would be rude. c "you'd better wait" is advice, not a request.

A, Yamada-san, watashi mo issho ni kaerimasu. Chotto matte kudasai.

Q3 Choose

あぶないよ。そんなにスピードを　。

It's dangerous! You'd better not go so fast.

Show answerHide answer
Answera. 出ださないほうがいいよ

Grammarない form + ほうがいい

Negative advice is always ～ないほうがいい. b ほうがよくない is not the pattern. c The negative uses the ない form, not the past ～なかった.

Abunai yo. Sonna ni supiido o dasanai hou ga ii yo.

Q4 Choose

大事だいじなやくそくだから、　。

It's an important appointment, so I'd better write it down on paper.

Show answerHide answer
Answerc. 紙かみに書かかないと

Grammar～ないと = "I've got to (do ~)"

～ないと means "I've got to do this." Writing it down is something you need to do: 書かかないと. a 忘わすれないと would mean "I've got to forget," and b おくれないと "I've got to be late," both the opposite of what you want.

Daiji na yakusoku da kara, kami ni kakanai to.

Q5 Choose

けん「お父とうさん、けがをしているときはおさけを　。」
父ちち「そうだな。」

Ken: "Dad, you'd better not drink alcohol while you're injured."
Father: "I suppose you're right."

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 飲のまないほうがいいよ

Grammarほうがいい (advice)

A child is advising his father: "you'd better not drink." a なさい is a parent's order to a child, not the other way around, and the meaning ("drink!") is wrong. c 飲のまないと = "you've got to drink," the opposite.

Ken: "Otousan, kega o shite iru toki wa osake o nomanai hou ga ii yo."
Chichi: "Sou da na."

Q6 Choose

けん「お母かあさん、もう7時じだよ。」
母はは「ご飯はんを　ね。ちょっと待まっていて。」

Ken: "Mom, it's already 7 o'clock."
Mother: "I've got to make dinner, haven't I. Wait a little."

Show answerHide answer
Answerc. 作つくらないと

Grammar～ないと (pushing yourself to act)

The mother realizes she needs to make dinner: 作つくらないと. a would be ordering Ken to cook. b "better not make it" doesn't fit.

Haha: "Gohan o tsukuranai to ne. Chotto matte ite."

Q7 Choose

たかし君くん、そんなに　。ぼくが悪わるかったよ。ごめんね。

Takashi, please don't be so angry. It was my fault. Sorry.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. おこらないで

Grammar～ないで (please don't ~)

The speaker is asking Takashi not to be angry: おこらないで. a おこって = "get angry!" c おこらないと = "you've got to get angry," the opposite.

Takashi-kun, sonna ni okoranaide. Boku ga warukatta yo. Gomen ne.

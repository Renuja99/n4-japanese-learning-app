Problem 115 questions
Instructions

　に何なんを入いれますか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in the ( )? Choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

この花はなはあのまるいテーブルの上うえ　かざりましょう。

Let's put these flowers on top of that round table.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. に

Grammarに (the place where something ends up)

かざる ("decorate / put on display") takes に for where the thing is placed. 1 で is where an action happens, not where something is put. 3 を is already used for 花はな (here shown with は). 4 が marks the subject.

Kono hana wa ano marui teeburu no ue ni kazarimashou.

Q2 Choose

旅行会社りょこうかいしゃの人ひと「あしたは10時じにこのホテル　出発しゅっぱつして、一日中いちにちじゅう町まちを見物けんぶつします。」

Travel agent: "Tomorrow we'll leave this hotel at 10 and spend the whole day sightseeing around town."

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. を

Grammarを (the place you leave)

With "leave" verbs (出発しゅっぱつする, 出でる), を marks the place you leave. 2 で is where an action happens. 3 へ and 4 に mark a destination, not a starting point.

Ashita wa juuji ni kono hoteru o shuppatsu shite, ichinichijuu machi o kenbutsu shimasu.

Q3 Choose

これは　あげられません。大切たいせつな人ひとからもらった物ものなんです。

I can't give this to anyone. It's something I got from someone important to me.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. だれにも

Grammarだれ + に + も + negative (not to anyone)

あげる needs に for the person you give to, and も + negative means "not to anyone": だれにも…ません. 1 一人ひとりも is missing に. 2 一人ひとりでも = "even one person." 4 だれでも = "anyone at all" (goes with positive verbs).

Kore wa dare ni mo ageraremasen. Taisetsu na hito kara moratta mono na n desu.

Q4 Choose

わあ、　セーターですね。色いろもいいし……。

Wow, that sweater looks warm. And the colour's nice too…

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. あたたかそうな

Grammar～そう (looks ～, based on what you see)

The speaker is looking at the sweater and judging by its appearance: あたたかそうな. 1 らしい = "I hear." 3 みたいな is a guess, but sounds odd for what you can see right in front of you. 4 はず = "should be (by logic)."

Waa, atatakasou na seetaa desu ne. Iro mo ii shi…

Q5 Choose

A「この写真しゃしんの人ひとを知しっていますか。」
B「うーん、どこかで1、2度ど　あるんですが、思おもい出だせません。」

A: "Do you know the person in this photo?"
B: "Hmm, I've met them once or twice somewhere, but I can't remember."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 会あったことが

Grammar～たことがある (have done before)

Past experience: 会あったことがある. 2 会あうことがある = "sometimes meet." 1 and 3 の can't be used in this pattern.

B: "Uun, dokoka de ichi, nido atta koto ga aru n desu ga, omoidasemasen."

Q6 Choose

おいしそうなケーキがありますね……。じゃ、ダイエットはあしたからに　と思おもいます。

That cake looks delicious… OK, I think I'll start my diet tomorrow instead.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. しよう

GrammarVolitional + と思おもう (I think I'll ～)

A decision the speaker is making: volitional しよう + と思おもいます. 1 するとおもいます is a guess or prediction. 3 is ongoing. 4 isn't a complete form before と.

Oishisou na keeki ga arimasu ne… Ja, daietto wa ashita kara ni shiyou to omoimasu.

Q7 Choose

A「あれ、めがねはどこにおいたんだろう。」
B「めがねなら、ここにおいて　。」

A: "Huh, where did I put my glasses?"
B: "If it's your glasses, they've been put here."

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. ありますよ

Grammar～てある (result of someone's action)

Someone put the glasses there, and they're still there: おいてあります. 2 ～てくる and 4 ～ていく are about movement. 3 ～ておく = "do in advance," which is a future action.

B: "Megane nara, koko ni oite arimasu yo."

Q8 Choose

A「600字じいないで作文さくぶんを書かいてください。」
B「ああ、よかった。　んですね。」

A: "Please write the essay in 600 characters or fewer."
B: "Oh, good. So it doesn't need to be 800 characters."

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 800字じでなくてもいい

Grammar～なくてもいい (don't have to)

B is relieved (よかった), so it's shorter than expected: "it doesn't have to be 800." 2 "must be 800" isn't a relief. 3 "800 is OK" contradicts the 600 limit. 4 "800 isn't allowed" isn't what makes them glad.

B: "Aa, yokatta. Happyaku-ji de nakute mo ii n desu ne."

Q9 Choose

A「来週らいしゅう、田中たなかさんのけっこんしきに行いくんです。」
B「けっこんしきに　、白しろい服ふくを着きていかないほうがいいですよ。」

A: "I'm going to Tanaka's wedding next week."
B: "If you're going to a wedding, you'd better not wear white."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 行いくなら

Grammar～なら (if that's the case, here's advice)

B picks up what A said and gives advice: 行いくなら. The advice (what to wear) comes before going. 2 行いったら = "after you've gone," too late to choose clothes. 1 と can't be followed by advice. 4 ば doesn't fit this "picking up the topic" use.

B: "Kekkonshiki ni iku nara, shiroi fuku o kite ikanai hou ga ii desu yo."

Q10 Choose

A「今度こんどのパーティーにはぜんぶで何人なんにん来きますか。」
B「何人なんにんに　まだわからないんですが、20人にんぐらいだと思おもいます。」

A: "How many people are coming to the next party in total?"
B: "I don't know yet how many it'll be, but I think about 20."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. なるか

GrammarQuestion word + ～か (embedded question)

A question with 何人なんにん inside a sentence ends in か: 何人なんにんになるかわからない. 1, 2 and 3 don't form an embedded question.

B: "Nannin ni naru ka mada wakaranai n desu ga, nijuunin gurai da to omoimasu."

Q11 Choose

さいきん、となりの家いえからピアノの音おとが　なりました。

Recently, I've started to hear piano music from next door.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 聞きこえるように

Grammar～ようになる (a change: came to ～)

音おとが (subject) + 聞きこえる ("can be heard"), and a change over time: ようになりました. 1 聞きく needs を. 3 and 4 ことになる is for decisions made by others.

Saikin, tonari no ie kara piano no oto ga kikoeru you ni narimashita.

Q12 Choose

A「たくさんにもつがありますね。一ひとつ　。」
B「すみません。おねがいします。」

A: "You've got a lot of bags. Shall I carry one?"
B: "Thank you. Yes, please."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 持もちましょうか

Grammar～ましょうか (offering help)

A offers to help: 持もちましょうか ("Shall I carry it?"). 1 asks B to carry. 2 asks if B will carry. 3 ～ませんか is an invitation to do something together.

A: "Takusan nimotsu ga arimasu ne. Hitotsu mochimashou ka."

Q13 Choose

今日きょうは　電車でんしゃがこんでいますね。何なんかあったんでしょうか。

The train is more crowded than usual today. I wonder if something happened.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. いつもより

Grammar～より (more than)

Comparing today with usual: いつもより ("than usual"). 2 ほど needs a negative after it. 3 いつもなら = "normally, (but)…" 4 isn't natural.

Kyou wa itsumo yori densha ga konde imasu ne. Nanika atta n deshou ka.

Q14 Choose

A「あ、めずらしい魚さかなね。自分じぶんで料理りょうりするの？だいじょうぶ？」
B「だいじょうぶ。店員てんいんさんに料理りょうりのし方かたをよく　買かったから。」

A: "Oh, that's an unusual fish. Are you going to cook it yourself? Will you be OK?"
B: "I'll be fine. I asked the shop assistant carefully how to cook it before I bought it."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 聞きいてから

Grammar～てから (after doing)

First asked, then bought: 聞きいてから. 1 あげる = doing it for someone else. 2 くれる = someone did it for B. 3 passive = "after being asked."

B: "Daijoubu. Ten'in-san ni ryouri no shikata o yoku kiite kara katta kara."

Q15 Choose

そんなに　考かんがえないで、まずやってみてください。

Don't think about it so seriously; just try it first.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. むずかしく

Grammarい-adjective → adverb with く

Before the verb 考かんがえる you need an adverb: むずかしく ("in a difficult way"). 1 isn't a real form. 2 is a noun. 4 links clauses.

Sonna ni muzukashiku kangaenaide, mazu yatte mite kudasai.

Problem 25 questions
Instructions

★に入はいるものはどれですか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

Which piece goes in the ★ slot? Put the four pieces in the correct order and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q16 Choose

A「おいしかったね、あの店みせ。また行いこうね。」
B「あ！店みせにけいたい電話でんわをおいて 　 　 ★ 　 と。」

A: "That restaurant was good. Let's go again."
B: "Oh! I left my phone at the restaurant, so I need to go back for it."

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. とりに (order: 3 → 1 → 4 → 2)

Grammar～てくる / ～てしまう / stem + に (purpose) / ～ないと (must)

Full sentence: 店みせにけいたい電話でんわをおいてきて しまったから とりに もどらないと。 ★ is the third slot: とりに.

B: "A! Mise ni keitai denwa o oite kite shimatta kara tori ni modoranai to."

Q17 Choose

A「あれ？この本ほんは何なんですか。かたづけてもいいですか。」
B「いえ、　 　 ★ 　 あるんです。」

A: "Huh? What's this book? Can I put it away?"
B: "No, I've put it out so I don't forget it tomorrow."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. ように (order: 4 → 1 → 3 → 2)

Grammar～ないように (so as not to) / ～てある

Full sentence: いえ、あした わすれない ように 出だしてあるんです。 ★ is the third slot: ように.

B: "Ie, ashita wasurenai you ni dashite aru n desu."

Q18 Choose

これはこのしまで 　 　 ★ 　 そうですよ。

I hear this is a rare bird that can only be seen on this island.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. めずらしい (order: 4 → 2 → 1 → 3)

Grammar～しか～ない (only) / plain form + そうだ (I hear)

Full sentence: これはこのしまでしか 見みられない めずらしい 鳥とりだそうですよ。 ★ is the third slot: めずらしい.

Kore wa kono shima de shika mirarenai mezurashii tori da sou desu yo.

Q19 Choose

なかなか出でかける 　 　 ★ 　 しまいました。

I just couldn't get ready to go out, and I ended up being late.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. おそく (order: 4 → 1 → 2 → 3)

Grammarなかなか～ない / ～くなる / ～てしまう

Full sentence: なかなか出でかけるじゅんびが できなくて おそく なってしまいました。 ★ is the third slot: おそく.

Nakanaka dekakeru junbi ga dekinakute osoku natte shimaimashita.

Q20 Choose

わたしたちが会あった 　 　 ★ 　 すぐに友ともだちになりました。

That was the first time we'd ever met, but we became friends straight away.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. はじめてだった (order: 4 → 2 → 1 → 3)

Grammar～のは…が / ～のに (even though)

Full sentence: わたしたちが会あったのは そのときが はじめてだった のにすぐに友ともだちになりました。 ★ is the third slot: はじめてだった.

Watashi-tachi ga atta no wa sono toki ga hajimete datta noni sugu ni tomodachi ni narimashita.

Problem 35 questions
Instructions

21 から 25 に何なんを入いれますか。文章ぶんしょうの意味いみを考かんがえて、1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in blanks 21 to 25? Think about the meaning of the passage and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

The passage: こけし (Kokeshi dolls), by Sarah Smith

先月せんげつある町まちに旅行りょこうに行いったとき、「こけし」[21] おみやげを買かいました。こけしは木きの人形にんぎょうで、大おおきさはいろいろありましたが、わたしが [22]、せの高たかさが25センチぐらいの物ものです。手ても足あしも首くびもないまっすぐな体からだの上うえに小ちいさいボールのような頭あたまが [23]。それだけの形かたちの人形にんぎょうですが、とてもやさしい顔かおをしています。細ほそい目めと小ちいさい口くちが特とくにいいです。体からだには赤あかい花はながかいてあります。どのこけしもみんな赤あかい色いろが使つかわれていました。わたしは店みせの人ひとに「[24] ですか。」と聞きいてみました。店みせの人ひとの話はなしでは、赤あかは病気びょうきを体からだに入いれない色いろなのだそうです。

この町まちは特とくにこけしのおみやげが有名ゆうめいで、むかしからよく売うられていたようです。でも、[25]、こけしはここだけではなく、日本にほん中なかのいろいろな所ところで買かうことができます。

Translation: Last month, when I went on a trip to a certain town, I bought a souvenir [21] called a "kokeshi." Kokeshi are wooden dolls. They came in all sizes, but [22] the one I bought is about 25 cm tall. On a straight body with no arms, legs or neck, there [23] is a small, ball-like head. It's a doll with just that shape, but it has a very gentle face. The thin eyes and small mouth are especially nice. Red flowers are painted on the body. Every kokeshi used red. I asked the shop assistant, "[24] Why is that?" According to them, red is a colour that keeps illness out of the body. This town is especially famous for kokeshi souvenirs, and it seems they've been sold here for a long time. But [25] nowadays, you can buy kokeshi not only here but in many places all over Japan.

Q21 Choose

「こけし」[21] おみやげを買かいました。

I bought a souvenir called a "kokeshi."

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. という

GrammarAというB (a B called A)

Naming something: こけしというおみやげ. 2, 3 and 4 are past or passive forms of "say," which don't work for simply giving a name.

"Kokeshi" to iu omiyage o kaimashita.

Q22 Choose

わたしが [22]、せの高たかさが25センチぐらいの物ものです。

The one I bought is about 25 cm tall.

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 買かったのは

Grammar～のは…です / past tense

She bought it last month, a single finished action: 買かったのは. 1 is future/habit. 2 and 3 are ongoing forms, which don't fit a one-off purchase.

Watashi ga katta no wa, se no takasa ga nijuugo-senchi gurai no mono desu.

Q23 Choose

…小ちいさいボールのような頭あたまが [23]。

…there's a small ball-like head (attached).

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. ついています

GrammarIntransitive + ている (a state)

She's describing what the doll looks like now: the head is attached → ついています. 1 "got attached" is a one-time event. 3 and 4 are about movement or change over time.

…chiisai booru no you na atama ga tsuite imasu.

Q24 Choose

わたしは店みせの人ひとに「[24] ですか。」と聞きいてみました。

I asked the shop assistant, "Why is that?"

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. どうして

Grammarどうして (why)

She wants to know the reason all kokeshi are red, and the answer is a reason ("red keeps illness out"). 1 どう = "how is it?" 3 どんなこと = "what kind of thing?" 4 どうやって = "by what method?"

Watashi wa mise no hito ni "Doushite desu ka." to kiite mimashita.

Q25 Choose

むかしからよく売うられていたようです。でも、[25]、こけしはここだけではなく…買かうことができます。

It seems they've been sold here since long ago. But nowadays, you can buy them all over Japan, not just here.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 今いまでは

Grammar今いまでは (nowadays, unlike before)

It contrasts the past (only this town) with the present (everywhere): 今いまでは. 1 今いまも and 2 今いまでも mean "still, as before," but the situation has changed. 4 今いまから = "from now on."

Demo, ima de wa, kokeshi wa koko dake de wa naku, Nihonjuu no iroiro na tokoro de kau koto ga dekimasu.

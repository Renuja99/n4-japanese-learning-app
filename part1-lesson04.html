Grammar points on page 38

1

～

られ)ます: the potential form ("can ~"

  • Form: potential form of the verb (see book p.18). Group I: 話はなす → 話はなせる; Group II: 着きる → 着きられる; する → できる; 来くる → 来こられる.
  • Shows someone's ability (①②), that a situation makes something possible (③), or that something has a certain property (④). Used with verbs for things people do on purpose. The object particle を often changes to が: 中国語ちゅうごくごを話はなす → 中国語ちゅうごくごが話はなせる.
  • Examples from the book:
    1. ジョーさんは英語えいごと日本にほん語ごと中国語ちゅうごくごが話はなせます。 Joe can speak English, Japanese and Chinese.
    2. はなちゃんはまだ一人ひとりで服ふくが着きられません。 Hana can't put on her clothes by herself yet.
    3. このびじゅつかんでは有名ゆうめいなえが見みられます。 You can see famous paintings at this art museum.
    4. この水みずは飲のめません。 This water isn't drinkable.
2

～ができます / ～ことができます

"can ~" / "be able to ~"

  • Form: noun + ができます; verb dictionary form + ことができます
  • Shows that something is possible or allowed (①②) or that someone has the ability (③). A slightly more formal way to say the same thing as the potential form.
  • Examples from the book:
    1. このコンビニでは24時間じかん買かい物ものができます。 You can shop at this convenience store 24 hours a day.
    2. 今いま、この建物たてものの中なかには入はいることができません。 You can't enter this building right now.
    3. わたしは日本にほんの県けんの名前なまえをぜんぶ言いうことができます。 I can say the names of all of Japan's prefectures.
3

見みえます・聞きこえます

can be seen / can be heard

  • Form: noun + が見みえます / が聞きこえます
  • Means something naturally comes into your eyes or ears, without trying. Compare: 見みられます / 聞きけます = you can see/hear it because the situation allows it (e.g. a museum, a website).
  • Examples from the book:
    1. いいへやですね。まどから海うみが見みえます。 Nice room. You can see the sea from the window.
    2. めがねがありませんから、よく見みえません。 I don't have my glasses, so I can't see well.
    3. 風かぜの音おとが聞きこえるね。 You can hear the sound of the wind, can't you?

Change the word in the brackets ( ) into the correct form and write it in the blank. For questions 1–6, use the potential form.

Q1 Write

ばんご飯はんは1時間じかんぐらいで　。（作つくる）

I can make dinner in about an hour.

Show answerHide answer
Answer作つくれます（作つくれる）

GrammarPotential form, Group I verb

作つくる is Group I: change the final う-sound to the え-sound and add る: 作つくる → 作つくれる → polite 作つくれます.

Bangohan wa ichijikan gurai de tsukuremasu.

Q2 Write

車くるまの運転うんてんが　人ひとをさがしています。（する）

We're looking for someone who can drive a car.

Show answerHide answer
Answerできる

GrammarPotential of する = できる

The potential form of する is できる. Because it's describing 人ひと (a person), it stays in the plain form: 運転うんてんができる人ひと = "a person who can drive."

Kuruma no unten ga dekiru hito o sagashite imasu.

Q3 Write

あの人ひとの名前なまえをわすれた。ぜんぜん　。（思おもい出だす）

I forgot that person's name. I can't remember it at all.

Show answerHide answer
Answer思おもい出だせない

GrammarPotential form (negative), Group I verb

思おもい出だす → 思おもい出だせる (す → せる). ぜんぜん ("not at all") needs a negative, so 思おもい出だせない. Plain form because the sentence is casual (わすれた).

Ano hito no namae o wasureta. Zenzen omoidasenai.

Q4 Write

これ、一人ひとりで　か。（持もっていく）

Can you carry this (and take it) by yourself?

Show answerHide answer
Answer持もっていけます

GrammarPotential form of ～ていく

Only the last verb changes: 持もっていく → 持もっていける → polite 持もっていけます + か.

Kore, hitori de motte ikemasu ka.

Q5 Write

何時なんどきまでここに　か。（いる）

Until what time can you stay here?

Show answerHide answer
Answerいられます

GrammarPotential form, Group II verb

いる is Group II, so add られる: いる → いられる → いられます.

Nanji made koko ni iraremasu ka.

Q6 Write

A「あした、朝あさ6時じにここに　か。」（来くる） B「はい、だいじょうぶです。」

A: "Can you come here at 6 tomorrow morning?"
B: "Yes, that's fine."

Show answerHide answer
Answer来こられます（こられます）

GrammarPotential form of 来くる

来くる is irregular: its potential form is 来こられる (こられる) → 来こられます.

A: "Ashita, asa rokuji ni koko ni koraremasu ka."
B: "Hai, daijoubu desu."

Q7 Write

夜よる、一人ひとりでこわい映画えいがを　ことができますか。（見みる）

Can you watch a scary movie alone at night?

Show answerHide answer
Answer見みる

GrammarDictionary form + ことができます

ことができる always follows the dictionary form, so just 見みる.

Yoru, hitori de kowai eiga o miru koto ga dekimasu ka.

Q8 Write

このクラブには、だれでも　ことができます。（入はいる）

Anyone can join this club.

Show answerHide answer
Answer入はいる

GrammarDictionary form + ことができます

Again, dictionary form before ことができる: 入はいることができます. (Here it's about being allowed to join.)

Kono kurabu ni wa, daredemo hairu koto ga dekimasu.

Instructions

Choose the best option.

Q1 Choose

となりのへやからわらっている声こえが　。

I can hear voices laughing from the next room.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 聞きこえます

Grammar聞きこえます (sound naturally reaches your ears)

You aren't trying to listen; the laughing just reaches your ears. That's 聞きこえる. a 聞きけます means you are able to listen (e.g. "you can listen to music on this site"). c 聞きこえる is already an "ability" verb, so it has no potential form; 聞きこえられる is not a word.

Tonari no heya kara waratte iru koe ga kikoemasu.

Q2 Choose

音おとが小ちいさいですから、よく　。

The sound is quiet, so I can't hear it well.

Show answerHide answer
Answerc. 聞きこえません

Grammar聞きこえません (can't hear naturally)

The sound is too quiet to reach your ears, a natural (in)ability, so 聞きこえません. a 聞ききません = "I don't listen" (a choice). b 聞きけません = "I'm not able to listen" because of a situation (e.g. no internet), not because the sound is quiet.

Oto ga chiisai desu kara, yoku kikoemasen.

Q3 Choose

わたしはときどき音楽おんがくを　ながら、勉強べんきょうします。

I sometimes study while listening to music.

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 聞きき

Grammarます-stem + ながら (with an intentional action)

Listening to music while studying is something you do on purpose, so use 聞きく → 聞きき + ながら. a 聞きこえ is not deliberate (and ながら needs an intentional action). c 聞きけ is the potential stem: "while being able to listen" doesn't make sense.

Watashi wa tokidoki ongaku o kikinagara, benkyou shimasu.

Q4 Choose

テレビがこわれているから、ドラマが　。

The TV is broken, so I can't watch the drama.

Show answerHide answer
Answerc. 見みられない

Grammar見みられる (can watch, because the situation allows) vs. 見みえる

The problem is the situation (the TV is broken), not your eyes, so the potential 見みられない. a 見みえない is about your eyes or something blocking your view. b 見みない = "I won't watch" (your choice).

Terebi ga kowarete iru kara, dorama ga mirarenai.

Q5 Choose

あれ？むこうにちょっと火ひが　。火事かじでしょうか。

Huh? I can see a bit of fire over there. Could it be a fire (a building on fire)?

Show answerHide answer
Answera. 見みえますね

Grammar見みえます (naturally comes into view)

The speaker wasn't trying to see the fire; it just came into view. That's 見みえる. b 見みますね = "I'll look / I watch," and the particle が doesn't fit. c 見みられます is for "you're able to see it (thanks to the situation)," like at a museum.

Are? Mukou ni chotto hi ga miemasu ne. Kaji deshou ka.

Q6 Choose

林はやしさんはおさけが　。

Can you drink alcohol, Mr./Ms. Hayashi?

Show answerHide answer
Answerb. 飲のめますか

GrammarPotential form + が

The particle が is the hint: with the potential form, を changes to が (おさけが飲のめる). a 飲のみます would need を (おさけを飲のみますか). c 飲のまれる is the passive/honorific form, not the potential of 飲のむ (which is 飲のめる).

Hayashi-san wa osake ga nomemasu ka.

Q7 Choose

大おおきい本ほんだなはたくさん本ほんが　。

A big bookshelf can hold a lot of books.

Show answerHide answer
Answera. 入はいります

Grammar入はいる (to fit / go in) as a property of a thing

The subject is books (本ほんが). Books "go in / fit" the shelf: 本ほんが入はいります. b 入いれます here would be 入いれる ("to put in"), which needs a person putting books with を. c 入はいることができます is for a person's intentional action, not an object.

Ookii hondana wa takusan hon ga hairimasu.

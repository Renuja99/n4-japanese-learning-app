Problem 16 questions
Instructions

　に何なんを入いれますか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in the ( )? Choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

ざんねんです。一ひとつ　答こたえをまちがえました。99てんです。

What a shame. I got just one answer wrong. I scored 99.

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. だけ

Grammarだけ (only) with a positive verb

"Only one wrong" with a positive verb (まちがえました) = だけ. 2 しか needs a negative. 1 一ひとつも needs a negative ("not even one"). 3 一ひとつでも ("even one") doesn't fit.

Zannen desu. Hitotsu dake kotae o machigaemashita. Kyuujuukyuu-ten desu.

Q2 Choose

A「運転手うんてんしゅさん、みんながまだ集あつまりません。出発しゅっぱつの時間じかん、だいじょうぶですか。」
B「えーと、8時じ　待まてますが……。8時じをすぎると、こまりますねえ。」

A: "Driver, everyone hasn't gathered yet. Is the departure time OK?"
B: "Um, I can wait until 8 o'clock, but… after 8 would be a problem."

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. までは

Grammarまで (until) + は (contrast/limit)

Waiting continues until 8, so まで; は adds "up to 8 is OK (but not after)." 2 and 4 までに is for a deadline for a one-time action. 3 まででは isn't natural here.

B: "Eeto, hachiji made wa matemasu ga…."

Q3 Choose

こうえんのそば　駅えきへ行いきましょう。そのほうが早はやいです。

Let's go to the station by passing beside the park. It's faster that way.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. を通とおって

Grammarを = route you pass along

"Go via the side of the park": そばを通とおって. 1 "leave the side of the park" is odd. 3 "arrive beside the park" changes the route. 4 で marks where an action happens, not a route.

Kouen no soba o tootte eki e ikimashou. Sono hou ga hayai desu.

Q4 Choose

けん「お母かあさん、おなかがすいたよ。」
母はは「わかった。じゃ、サンドイッチ　。」

Ken: "Mum, I'm hungry."
Mum: "OK. Then shall I make a sandwich or something?"

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. でも作つくろうか

Grammarでも = soft suggestion

でも ("or something") goes with an offer: 作つくろうか. 2 でも doesn't fit a past statement. 3 and 4 にも don't make sense with サンドイッチ here.

Haha: "Wakatta. Ja, sandoicchi demo tsukurou ka."

Q5 Choose

A「この本ほん、おもしろいですよ。読よんでみませんか。」
B「あ、その本ほんならもう　読よみました。」

A: "This book is interesting. Why don't you try reading it?"
B: "Oh, if it's that book, I've already read it many times."

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 何度なんども

Grammar何なん + counter + も = many times

何度なんども = "many times." 1 何度なんど alone is a question ("how many times?"). 3 一度いちども needs a negative. 4 一度いちどで means "in one go."

B: "A, sono hon nara mou nando mo yomimashita."

Q6 Choose

A「ここはたばこがすえる場所ばしょですか。」
B「ええ、たばこは　すえないんです。ほかの所ところではだめです。」

A: "Is this a place where you can smoke?"
B: "Yes, you can only smoke here. It's not allowed anywhere else."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. ここでしか

Grammarしか + negative = only

The verb is negative (すえない), and the meaning is "only here." That's しか. 4 だけ would need a positive verb (ここだけですえる). 1 "even here" and 2 "here (contrast)" don't mean "only."

B: "Ee, tabako wa koko de shika suenai n desu. Hoka no tokoro de wa dame desu."

Problem 23 questions
Instructions

★に入はいるものはどれですか。1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

Which piece goes in the ★ slot? Put the four pieces in the correct order and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

Q1 Choose

この村むら 　 　 ★ 　 生いきている人ひとがいます。

In this village there's a person who has lived the longest in Japan.

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. で (order: 3 → 1 → 4 → 2)

Grammarに (location of existence) / で (scope of comparison)

Full sentence: この村むらに 日本にほん で いちばん長ながく生いきている人ひとがいます。 "In this village (に, existence) there's a person who has lived longest in Japan (で, scope)." ★ is the third slot: で.

Kono mura ni Nihon de ichiban nagaku ikite iru hito ga imasu.

Q2 Choose

さあ、あちらの 　 　 ★ 　 用意よういしましたから、どうぞ。

Now, I've prepared tea on the table over there, so please help yourself.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. お茶ちゃ (order: 1 → 3 → 2 → 4)

Grammarに (place) / を (object)

Full sentence: あちらのテーブル に お茶ちゃ を用意よういしました。 ★ is the third slot: お茶ちゃ.

Saa, achira no teeburu ni ocha o youi shimashita kara, douzo.

Q3 Choose

A「今年ことしの秋あきにはおいしい米こめがたくさんできそうですね。」
B「はい。少すこし 　 　 ★ 　 と思おもいます。」

A: "It looks like lots of good rice will be grown this autumn."
B: "Yes. I hope that as much good rice as possible is produced, even just a little more."

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. いいのができると (order: 4 → 2 → 3 → 1)

Grammar少すこしでも (even a little) / ～といい (hope)

Full sentence: 少すこしでも 多おおく いいのができると いいと思おもいます。 ★ is the third slot: いいのができると.

B: "Hai. Sukoshi demo ooku ii no ga dekiru to ii to omoimasu."

Problem 34 questions
Instructions

1 から 4 に何なんを入いれますか。文章ぶんしょうの意味いみを考かんがえて、1・2・3・4からいちばんいいものを一ひとつえらんでください。

What goes in blanks 1 to 4? Think about the meaning of the passage and choose the best option from 1, 2, 3 and 4.

The passage ("Mt. Fuji" by Tom Brown):

先週せんしゅう、学校がっこうの旅行りょこうで富士山ふじさんに行いきました。今いままで写真しゃしんやテレビでは見みたことがありますが、はじめてほんとうの富士山ふじさんが見みられて、うれしかったです。

むかし、富士山ふじさんはこわい山やまだと思おもわれていたようです。山やまがおこると、[1] 出でます。ですから、むかしの人ひとは山やまがおこらないように、[2] 富士山ふじさんにむかって手てを合あわせて、頭あたまを下さげたのだそうです。

わたしたちは富士山ふじさんのいちばん上うえまでのぼろうと思おもいました。でも、急きゅうに天気てんきが悪わるくなって、半分はんぶんまでしか行いけませんでした。富士山ふじさんの [3] 見みえる朝日あさひはすばらしいそうです。国くにへ帰かえる前まえに [4]。

Translation: Last week I went to Mt. Fuji on a school trip. I'd seen it in photos and on TV before, but I was happy to see the real Mt. Fuji for the first time. Long ago, it seems Mt. Fuji was thought of as a frightening mountain. When the mountain gets angry, [1] fire comes out of the mountain. So people long ago, so that the mountain wouldn't get angry, [2] even from far away, faced Mt. Fuji, put their hands together and bowed their heads, I'm told. We planned to climb to the very top of Mt. Fuji. But the weather suddenly turned bad, and we could only get halfway. I hear the sunrise you can see [3] from the top of Mt. Fuji is wonderful. Before I go back to my country, [4] I want to see it once.

Q1 Choose

山やまがおこると、[1] 出でます。

When the mountain gets angry, fire comes out of the mountain.

Show answerHide answer
Answer2. 山やまから火ひが

Grammarから (from) / が (subject of an event)

Fire comes out from the mountain: 山やまから火ひが出でます. 1 まで = "up to." 3 and 4 add でも/も, which don't fit.

Yama ga okoru to, yama kara hi ga demasu.

Q2 Choose

むかしの人ひとは山やまがおこらないように、[2] 富士山ふじさんにむかって手てを合あわせて、頭あたまを下さげたのだそうです。

So that the mountain wouldn't get angry, people long ago, even from far away, faced Mt. Fuji, put their hands together and bowed.

Show answerHide answer
Answer4. 遠とおくからでも

Grammarから (from) + でも (even)

"Even from far away" = 遠とおくからでも. 1 and 3 まで = "as far as," which doesn't fit facing the mountain. 2 からでは means "judging from far away."

…tooku kara demo Fujisan ni mukatte te o awasete, atama o sageta no da sou desu.

Q3 Choose

富士山ふじさんの [3] 見みえる朝日あさひはすばらしいそうです。

I hear the sunrise you can see from the top of Mt. Fuji is wonderful.

Show answerHide answer
Answer3. 上うえから

Grammarから (viewpoint / from)

You see the sunrise from the top: 上うえから見みえる. 1 "even on top." 2 "only the top." 4 "up to the top."

Fujisan no ue kara mieru asahi wa subarashii sou desu.

Q4 Choose

国くにへ帰かえる前まえに [4]。

Before I go back to my country, I want to see it once.

Show answerHide answer
Answer1. 一度いちど見みたいです

GrammarExpressing a wish (たい)

He couldn't reach the top, so he wants to see the sunrise someday: 一度いちど見みたいです. 2 "never see." 3 "only see once" (odd). 4 is past, but he hasn't seen it.

Kuni e kaeru mae ni ichido mitai desu.
